Date: 2026-11-17. Desk research of public official sites, documentation and developer-authored app-store descriptions. No accounts created, paid subscriptions purchased or end-to-end product journeys tested. Product claims are not independently verified. Absence from documentation does not prove absence from a product.

## Interpretation

The broad all-in-one AI life tracking proposition already exists. Custom trackers, voice input and cross-domain insights are also represented. The strongest unverified differentiation hypothesis is a complete, explainable and editable event dependency system connecting arbitrary hobbies to actual/estimated health metrics, recurring obligations, travel costs and adaptive routines.

Separate four capabilities in product evaluation:
1. Store different domains together.
2. Identify statistical relationships.
3. Apply explicit rules consistently across domains, including edit/delete reversal.
4. Propose feasible plan changes across competing goals with user approval.

No reviewed public source documents the full proposed BJJ scenario end to end. This is an evidence gap, not proof that competitors cannot do it. Correlation also does not establish causation.

## Recommended next validation

Test KeeperPal, LifesOS, LifeOS AI and Visualife against the same scenario: create BJJ with custom fields; set weekly duration and recurring membership; log a shorter session and sparring; add travel mode; inspect calories/expenses/calendar effects; edit and delete the source event; request a change after a difficult week. Record manual steps, errors, provenance, corrections and time to useful result.

Start ReLog validation with one connected journey and target users already tracking training, spending and schedules in multiple apps. Measure reduced duplicate entry, correction effort, retained use and willingness to pay. Public competitor pricing is not evidence of conversion, market size or retention.
